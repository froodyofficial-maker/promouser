Settings that matter

Fine-tune your feel and control

Start with a sensible DPI, keep lift-off distance low if you want faster control, and adjust polling rate if your device supports it. Cable drag and surface quality can also change how your mouse feels.

For wireless devices, make sure you test battery life, connection stability, and sensor performance on your actual desk surface.

DPI

Choose a range that feels responsive without making aiming twitchy or inaccurate.

Polling rate

Higher polling rates improve tracking smoothness, especially in fast-paced games.

Surface

A good mouse mat helps reduce noise, improve glide, and keep tracking consistent.

Lift-off

Lower lift-off distance can help if you want your cursor to stay stable when you lift the mouse.
