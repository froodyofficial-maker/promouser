Bloody A70

A Minecraft favorite for drag clicking, bridging, and fast button response.

The Bloody A70 is a click-focused wired mouse that is popular with Minecraft bridgers. Its Light Strike switches and grippy button surface can support extremely high drag-click scores with the right technique, while its heavier body gives it a planted feel for movement-heavy play.

Back to Minecraft Mice Browse all mice

Drag clicking

Its button texture and Light Strike switches make it a well-known choice for players practising high-CPS drag-click techniques.

Planted feel

At 150g with its cable, it feels notably heavier than modern lightweight mice—useful for a steady feel, but less ideal for fast flicks.

Why it stands out

What to evaluate before buying

Its specialised click feel is the main draw, so make sure the weight and shape suit how you play.

Made for click practice

It makes the most sense for players who actively practise bridging or drag clicking, rather than for a lightweight all-rounder.

Polling rate and tracking

The A70 offers up to a 1,000Hz report rate, an adjustable 4,000 CPI sensor, and a wired connection for consistent input.

Settings

Start with a simple DPI and in-game sensitivity, then adjust only after you are comfortable with the mouse's heavier feel.

Next steps

Use the CPS Test and Minecraft Tips to practise and refine your technique.

ProMouser take

Who it fits best

Good fit if

You want a drag-clicking specialist for bridging and prefer a substantial, steady mouse over an ultralight shape.

Consider alternatives if

You need an ultralight mouse for fast PvP flicks, play for long sessions, or do not plan to use drag-click techniques.

Verdict

A specialist pick with exceptional drag-clicking potential. Confirm that you are comfortable with the weight before committing.
