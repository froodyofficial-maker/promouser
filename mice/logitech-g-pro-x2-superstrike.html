Logitech G PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE

A competitive mouse that puts click feel in your hands.

The Logitech G PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE is built around Logitech G's Haptic Inductive Trigger System (HITS), bringing adjustable click actuation and haptic feedback to the PRO lineup. It is designed for players who want to tune how their primary clicks feel and respond.
