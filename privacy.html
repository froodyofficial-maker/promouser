Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

ProMouser is a static website for mouse guides and browser-based tools. This policy explains what the site itself stores and what happens when you use external services linked from the site.

Information stored in your browser

The site does not require an account. A few features store small preferences locally in your browser:

  • The announcement banner dismissal is remembered for the current browser session using session storage.
  • The mouse comparison tool saves its selected comparison settings in local storage so they remain available on your device.
  • The recommendation assistant processes your questions in the page and reads the local mouse-guide pages to provide context. It does not send your chat text to an AI service.

These browser values are not sent to ProMouser. You can clear them through your browser’s site-data controls.

External services

Pages load typography from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to request those font files. Links to services such as YouTube, retailers, and Logitech open their own websites; those services have separate privacy practices that ProMouser does not control.

Analytics and advertising

ProMouser does not intentionally use its own analytics, advertising trackers, or account system on this static site.

Children and sensitive information

The site is not designed to collect personal or sensitive information. Please do not include private information in messages or reports submitted to third-party services.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated when the site’s features change. For questions, visit the contact page.
