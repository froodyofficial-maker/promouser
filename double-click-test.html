Double-click overview

Use it to spot timing issues early

A quick double-click check helps you spot whether your current mouse or hand position is adding strain or making consistent input harder. It is especially helpful when you are dialing in your weapon or switching to a new device.

The goal is not just speed, but stable and repeatable clicking without unnecessary tension.
