A versatile mouse with strong sensors, a heavy feel, and lots of customization.

ProMouser review

Owned, used, and experienced by ProMouser

What it does well

Great sensor, accurate tracking, and a very strong feature set for players who like custom buttons and extra control. It is a solid all-rounder for modern Minecraft PvP.

What to watch out for

This mouse is best for jitter clicking, not butterfly clicking. The buttons are smaller, and the default weight can feel heavy during fast movement, especially in Minecraft PvP.

Final verdict

For normal Minecraft and modern PvP, it is a great mouse. For older v1.8.9-style gameplay and fast clicking techniques, it is not the ideal choice.
